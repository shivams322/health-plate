Water

Plain water you log here, plus milk, chai, juice and other drinks from your meals.

Why water keeps your body and organs fit
  • Kidneys: enough fluid lets them flush out waste in urine and lowers the risk of kidney stones.
  • Body temperature: sweat cools you down. In Indian summers or a workout you can lose 0.5 to 1 litre an hour.
  • Digestion: water and fibre together keep stools soft and help prevent constipation.
  • Brain and energy: losing even 1 to 2% of body weight as water can bring tiredness, headache and poor focus.
  • Heart and blood: water keeps blood volume up, so the heart moves blood and nutrients with less strain.
  • Joints and skin: fluid cushions joints and keeps skin and the lining of your mouth and eyes moist.
  • Quick check: pale yellow urine means you're drinking enough. Dark yellow means drink more.
