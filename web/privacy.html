Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

In short: Health Plate has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything you log stays on your own phone or browser. We never see it.

What you enter

To set your targets and track your day, you can enter your sex, age, height, weight, daily routine, exercise, goal and diet preference. You also log the foods, drinks and water you have, with the time you ate. You can add your own foods.

Where it is stored

All of this is stored only on your device: in your browser's storage on the website, or in the app's private storage on Android and iOS. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. There is no Health Plate server that holds your data.

What we do not collect

We do not use analytics, advertising, cookies for tracking, or third-party trackers. The app's fonts and icons are bundled with it, so opening Health Plate does not contact other companies' servers.

Backups

You can download a backup file from My targets → Your data. That file is saved wherever you choose, such as your phone's files, Google Drive or iCloud. Keep it private, because it contains your health information. Restoring a backup replaces the data on that device.

Deleting your data

Use Clear this day to remove a day's log. To remove everything, uninstall the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Because we never hold a copy, deleted data cannot be recovered unless you kept a backup.

Health information

Health Plate gives general nutrition guidance for healthy adults, based on ICMR-NIN 2020 recommended dietary allowances and standard food composition data. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, have a medical condition or take medication, talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet.

Children

Health Plate is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on Health Plate's GitHub page.
